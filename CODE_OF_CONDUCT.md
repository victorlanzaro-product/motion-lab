# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Motion Lab to be welcoming and respectful to people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, use respectful language, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and work. Respect privacy. Do not post another person's identifying information, recordings, or biometric data without their informed permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private data or recordings without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may limit participation when needed. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact channel on the GitHub profile.
